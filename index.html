02 / The person behind the commits

I like the
whole picture.

I'm Dhanush, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate who enjoys building things that make someone's day a little easier.

I enjoy the mix of thoughtful frontend development, backend engineering, databases, and system architecture. I don't just want to make something that works. I want to understand why it works, who it helps, and how it can improve.

From building full-stack applications to working with industrial telemetry and exploring distributed systems, I'm happiest when I'm learning something new and turning it into something tangible.

I'm currently focused on growing as a software engineer, strengthening my fundamentals, and building products that combine a good user experience with solid engineering.

What drives me Curiosity, solving meaningful problems, and the satisfaction of turning an idea into something people can actually use.
